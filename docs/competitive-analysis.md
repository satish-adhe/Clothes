## 2. Key Differentiation Opportunities for NAMECLOTHES

1. **Integrated Procedural 3D Garment Engine**:
   - Unlike static photo-only stores or heavy 50MB GLB downloads that stall mobile devices, NAMECLOTHES provides a lightweight procedural WebGL 3D garment inspection engine (featuring the signature **Axiom Kinetic Trench**, **Monolith Puffer**, and **Vesper Drape Jacket**).
   - Real-time studio softbox reflections (`RoomEnvironment`), metallic hardware anisotropy, and customizable drape angles.
   - Built-in fallback for low-power devices and full `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.

2. **Editorial Campaign Meets Frictionless Commerce**:
   - Curated scenes: Scene 01 (Cinematic Reveal), Scene 02 (3D Interactive Flagship), Scene 03 (Featured Capsule), Scene 04 (Editorial Monograph), Scene 05 (New Silhouettes), Scene 06 (Philosophy & Atelier Craft).
   - Global persistent shopping state: Wishlist, Drawer Cart, Instant Checkout with demo-mode simulations and live payment architecture readiness.

3. **Complete Omnichannel Digital Experience**:
   - 14 dedicated routes: All collections, category filtering, individual product monographs, customer portal, administrative content management, verified terms & sustainability statements.
   - Robust offline-ready local persistence + role-based administrative catalog management.
